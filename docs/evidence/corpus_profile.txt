--- 5. CHUNK ID ORIGIN ANALYSIS (PUZZLE 2 REQUEST LOG) ---
In reference_hardware_and_model.csv and request_log.csv, cached chunk citations follow the pattern:
  Format: `<filing_id>#c<chunk_index>` (e.g., 'HBS-10K-FY2024#c1', 'ARG-10Q-FY2024Q1#c2')
  * Prefix: The canonical SEC filing ID (matching documents.doc_id and filings.filing_id).
  * Separator: '#' anchor delimiter standard in citation systems.
  * Suffix: 'c' followed by 1-based sequential integer indexing the chunk within that document.
Decision: Adopt this exact deterministic convention for text chunks `<doc_id>#c<index>`, and
`<company>_<fy>Q<fq>_<statement>#t<index>` for structured table chunks.

--- 6. CHUNKING STRATEGY RATIONALE & PARAMETER SELECTION ---
Key Data Characteristics:
  * Document Length: Mean 211 words (~1,300 chars); maximum document is 288 words (~1,700 chars).
  * SEC documents in this corpus are concise narrative extracts focusing on MD&A and Risk Factors.
Strategy Decision:
  1. Section-Boundary Splitting: Documents split cleanly on '=== SECTION ===' delimiters.
     - Chunk 1: Header / Document Metadata (Filing date, period end, reporting unit).
     - Chunk 2: MD&A narrative and performance commentary.
     - Chunk 3: Segment breakdown table (if present).
     - Chunk 4: Risk Factors (ITEM 1A) and cautionary disclosures.
  2. Chunk Size Target: 100 - 250 words per chunk (approximately 500 - 1,200 characters).
     - Fits comfortably inside local embedding models (BGE-small context: 512 tokens; MiniLM: 256 tokens).
     - Preserves full section semantic coherence without arbitrary sentence cuts.
  3. Overlap Policy: Minimal / Section-Context Prepend (0 token sliding overlap, 100% semantic header).
     - In concise SEC filings, sliding window overlap creates duplicate chunks that degrade retrieval precision.
     - Instead, each chunk prepends a structured header `[{company} {doc_id} | {section}]: `.
     - This guarantees the embedding model sees the exact corporate and temporal context.
  4. Table Chunks: Materialized separately from effective_line_items (Rule R2 / Step 2).
     - One table chunk per company x period x statement with canonical values, units, and restatement notes.
  5. Chunker Versioning: Stamped as chunker_version = 'v1.0.0'.
